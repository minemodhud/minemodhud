Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Minemod hud • Effective Date: October 2026 • Public Compliance Disclosure

1. Age Eligibility & Scope (Strict 18+)

Minemod hud operates as an interactive sandbox companion, modification repository, and AI-assisted creation utility.

  • Strict 18+ Requirement: By accessing Minemod hud, you affirm that you are at least eighteen (18) years of age. We do not knowingly solicit, process, or persist data belonging to minors.
  • Mature Content & Horror Labeling: The platform hosts community-crafted content including dark lore and psychological gaming modifications. Any modifications containing intense horror or gore are mandatory to carry an explicit "18+" thumbnail label. Sexually explicit, profane, and illegal code are strictly prohibited.

2. Identity Management & Google OAuth

Authentication is handled exclusively via Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication:

  • Processed Metadata: When signing in, we ingest only basic voluntary identity attributes: Public Display Name, Primary Email Address, Firebase Unique Identifier (UID), and Public Profile Avatar URI.
  • Zero Credential Retention: We never request, intercept, parse, or store your personal Google passwords, keys, or two-factor tokens.

3. Community Profiles & AI Ingestion Control

  • User Profiles: Stores local preferences, downloaded mod packages, star reviews, and earned credits bound strictly to your cryptographic UID.
  • Verified Creators: Creators authorized to publish assets undergo administrative review. Creator identities and approved mods are displayed publicly for author attribution.
  • AI Reference Consent: Creators hold sovereign control. If a creator toggles off AI reference permissions, their submissions remain entirely isolated from machine learning databases and generative tools.

4. Conversational AI & Safety Auditing

AI assistant queries route securely through an encrypted backend proxy endpoint on Render:

  • Ephemeral Processing: Prompts dispatched for gameplay queries or pack building are processed ephemerally and are never repurposed for proprietary model training.
  • Chat Persistence Control: Users can toggle conversational memory on or off, and retain unilateral authority to permanently delete stored chat logs.
  • Administrative Security Logs: To mitigate automated spam, ensure credit security, and protect against malicious prompts, chat telemetry is audited in an administrative security ledger (admin_live_chats) strictly for system health, abuse mitigation, and fraud detection.

5. Advertisements & Diagnostics

Minemod hud integrates Google AdMob and Firebase diagnostics for runtime health and rewarded credit delivery:

Telemetry processes standard diagnostic parameters (Advertising Identifiers/GAID, device model, OS build numbers, and crash logs) solely for stability mitigation and fraud prevention.

6. Hardware & Sensor Restrictions

  • Zero Background Tracking: Absolute exclusion of GPS or network-based geo-tracking.
  • Zero Contact Ingestion: Zero access to device address books, SMS logs, or call records.
  • Sandboxed File Picking: Avatar selections and image prompts execute solely through sandboxed system document pickers. We maintain no continuous camera access, background audio recording, or full-disk scanning capabilities.

7. Payment Verification & 12-Digit UTR

Minemod hud functions as an independent web application and operates entirely outside proprietary app store billing APIs:

  • No Financial Data Storage: We do not solicit or store credit cards, debit cards, CVVs, or UPI PINs.
  • Manual UTR Ledger: Credit top-ups and VIP passes are fulfilled externally via standard UPI. Activations are validated through manual reconciliation of the user-provided 12-digit Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) against administrative banking records.

8. Account Deletion & 7-Day Grace Period

  • In-App Deletion Engine: Permanent deletion can be scheduled at any time via App Personalization Settings.
  • 7-Day Recovery Window: Accounts enter a 7-day grace window. Logging into your account during these 7 days instantly cancels the deletion request.
  • Permanent Purge: Once 7 days elapse without login, all personal credentials, download records, and cloud partitions are permanently and irreversibly destroyed.
